Location: Canmore Recreation Centre (1900 8 Ave)

- Season begins on Wednesday, September 2, 2026

- Season ends on Wednesday, March 28, 2027

2026-2027 Schedule:

Wednesdays
Beginners from 5:15 pm to 6:20 pm
Advanced/Masters from 6:20 pm to 7:45 pm

Sundays
Beginners from 5:30 pm to 6:45 pm
