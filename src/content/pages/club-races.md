In addition to attending our regular training sessions, all skaters are encouraged to participate in our Club Race Nights. This is a fun and encouraging environment for all athletes, beginners to advanced, to experience a race format. The races are included within the regular registration fees. Each skater usually has 3 races after the group warm up. Race distances are based on age and ability.

These competitions are held at the Thelma Crowe Arena in Canmore on the designated Sunday nights during the first practice that starts at 5:30pm. Family and friends are encouraged to come out and cheer everyone on.
