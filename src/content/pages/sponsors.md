The Banff/Canmore Speed Skating Club gratefully acknowledges the support of our partners and sponsors who help make youth sport accessible in the Bow Valley.

## True Sport Youth Fund

We are proud to receive support from the **True Sport Youth Fund**, administered by the Bow Valley True Sport and Recreation Council with funding from the Wim & Nancy Pauw Foundation. This fund helps community sport organizations keep youth sport inclusive and accessible in the Bow Valley.

Banff/Canmore Speed Skating Club is a member of True Sport and committed to the [True Sport Principles](https://truesportpur.ca/true-sport-principles): Go For It, Play Fair, Respect Others, Keep It Fun, Stay Healthy, Include Everyone, and Give Back.

## Every Kid Can Play

Thank you to the **Government of Alberta** for support through the **Every Kid Can Play** grant program. This provincial initiative addresses affordability and accessibility challenges to sport, physical activity, and recreation programs for Alberta kids and their families.

Learn more: [Every Kid Can Play Program](https://www.alberta.ca/every-kid-can-play-program)

## Save-On-Foods

Thank you to **Save-On-Foods** for generously sponsoring food support at our club races. Their community support helps make race days special for our skaters and families.
