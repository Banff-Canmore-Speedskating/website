## Saturday November 29th, 2025

Canmore Recreation Centre, 1900 – 8th Avenue, Canmore, Alberta

- Warm-Ups: 7:10AM – 7:40AM

- Coaches’ Meeting: 7:15AM

- Racing: 8:00AM – 4:00PM

Entry is through IceReg – https://icereg.ca/#!/events/canmore-ice-racing-challenge-2025

Entry Deadline: Tuesday November 25, 2024, at Midnight

We will be capping the number of skaters at 115.

We are a very small club and we can’t run this race without volunteers.  Please consider helping.

Volunteer form – https://volunteersignup.org/JQHJW
