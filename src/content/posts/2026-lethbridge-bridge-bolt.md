![Four Banff/Canmore skaters in club hoodies at the Lethbridge Bridge Bolt](/media/2026-lethbridge-bolt/team-hoodies.jpg)

Six of our skaters made the trip to Lethbridge for the Bridge Bolt on October 24–25, and they came home with two medals and a pile of personal bests.

## On the podium

Congratulations to **Evan** on a **silver medal** and **Emilie** on a **bronze**!

## Personal bests

Five of our six skaters set at least one personal best:

- **Evan** — 1500 m in **2:40.17**, more than **14 seconds** faster than before, a 500 m PB of 50.70 (2.3 s faster), and a first-ever 777 m in 1:25.27.
- **Blai** — 400 m in **1:00.89**, almost **10 seconds** faster, and 200 m in 28.66 (5.5 s faster), plus a first 300 m.
- **Lianne** — PBs in three distances: 400 m in 44.83 (3.7 s faster), 500 m in 1:01.14 and 800 m in 1:37.27.
- **Emilie** — PBs in three distances too: 200 m in 25.31, 400 m in 46.68 (1.9 s faster) and 500 m in 1:02.13.
- **Sofya** — a 200 m PB of 32.79.
- **Yang** skated season bests in all three distances.

Full times are on the [Speed Skating Alberta results database](https://results.speedskatingalberta.ca/clubpage.php?club=Banff%2FCanmore).
