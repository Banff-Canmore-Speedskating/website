Our club is running an online silent auction to support our skaters — and there are some fantastic items up for grabs!

## What it supports

Proceeds go towards training materials and equipment for our skaters, plus general club operations like ice time, skates and skin suits.

## How it works

The auction runs online through SilentBid (code DVHQ). Browse the items, place your bids, and check back — bidding stays open until **Saturday, December 5, 2026 at 2:00 PM MST**.

<p><a class="cta" href="https://www.silentbid.ca/a/banff-canmore-speed-skating-club-silent-auction">Bid now &rarr;</a></p>

## Pickup details

Winning items must be picked up on **Saturday, December 5, 2–4 PM** in the **Main Lobby of the Canmore Rec Centre**. There's no shipping, so if you can't be there, have a family member, friend or fellow club member collect your items.
