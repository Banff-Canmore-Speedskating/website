![The whole club in our new blue hoodies at Club Race Night #1](/media/2026-race-night-1/team.jpg)

What a night! Our first club race night of the season had skaters of every age and level on the start line — from first-time racers to our masters — with parents and friends cheering from the boards. It was also the debut of our new blue club hoodies, and the team photo says it all.

Thank you to every skater who raced, and to the coaches, timers, officials and volunteers who made the evening run so smoothly (and to whoever brought the pizza).

## Want to race next time?

Club race nights are open to all of our skaters, and new skaters can join any time through the season. We skate Wednesdays and Sundays at the Canmore Rec Centre — see [our programs](/programs/) or come to a [free Try-It night](/try-it/).
